## 01-30-2026

Recently, I've spent some time thinking about the mental blocks I've been struggling with for the past year or so. Probably the biggest one that was preventing me from moving forward was a fear of making to the wrong decision, or failing at whatever it was I chose to do. Analysis paralysis is *real*.

Then I remembered my wise mentor Ryan once told me to "have a bias towards action." It makes a lot of sense, because if you're not doing *something*, you're doing nothing, and I realized how silly it was to be afraid of making mistakes or failing, because I definitely won't be improving if I'm doing nothing.

Now my goal is not to overanalyze what the right choice is, but to keep doing things and making mistakes and failing as much as possible, because anyways, as they say, some lessons are best learned the hard way.
